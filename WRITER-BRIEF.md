# Writer Working Brief

Last updated: 2 October 2026 (Asia/Jakarta)

## Roles and workflow

- Writer drafts website copy and stories first, using the positioning direction in this brief.
- The Designer owns visual direction, UX structure, information architecture, navigation, and marketing-side design decisions. The Designer works with Writer's draft and agrees the final structure before implementation.
- Writer refines the final copy within that structure. The Engineer owns code, builds, preview deployments, and approved releases.
- Writer is also responsible for turning Radhi's raw experiences into worthwhile stories about improving lead quality.
- The repository also contains a local skill package under `ai-skills` (identified by Radhi as `marketingskills-main`). Writer must inspect and follow its relevant instructions before beginning architecture-support or copywriting work.
- Relevant installed marketing skills should also be used according to the task, especially `site-architecture`, `copywriting`, `content-strategy`, `product-marketing`, and `cro`.

- Show a compact four-step lead flow beneath the headline: `Paid Ads`, `Lead Qualification`, `Follow-Up`, `Trial Booking`. It should guide attention toward the primary CTA.

- Do not show supporting copy beneath the CTA or the former three-item services row.

The homepage stays focused on the hero and primary enquiry CTA. Its header provides three paths:

1. Case Studies.
2. Blog.

The Working Together page remains available at `/how-i-work/` without a header link.

## Case study structure

- Organize each project as The Challenge, What I Did, The Work, and The Outcome.
- Use screenshots only as evidence within the story, never as a standalone gallery.
- Do not invent proof, metrics, screenshots, outcomes, or personal experience. Anonymize sensitive material.
- The current SaaS for Doctors study has approved challenge, approach, work steps, and results copy that can support this structure without new claims.

## Indonesian status

- Preserve existing Indonesian URLs. The new English header shows an unavailable ID option until its visual treatment is approved.
- Keep Indonesian copy semantically aligned in natural language. The existing Indonesian homepage and Working Together routes use corresponding headlines; their full design and navigation remain postponed.

## Blog relationship

- The blog is a separate content experience reached through the English header.
- Visitors on the blog or an individual blog post may navigate back to the homepage, Case Studies, or Contact.
- Blog index and article pages use the site's light editorial palette, Newsreader headings, Source Sans 3 body text, blue links, and yellow accents in both languages.

## Editorial direction

- Primary subject: how businesses can improve lead quality.
- Raw material: Radhi's real stories, observations, and operating experience.
- Distinctive territory: what happens after ads generate a lead, including qualification, WhatsApp conversations, CS follow-up, and trial bookings.
- Positioning foundation: April Dunford.
- Customer psychology and story angles: Katelyn Bourgoin.
- Concise, concrete presentation: Harry Dry / Marketing Examples.
- Research discipline and message validation: Peep Laja.
- These references are ingredients, not templates to imitate. The writing must retain Radhi's own voice and reflect the Indonesian education-business context.

## Current positioning direction

Radhi is a lead-generation practitioner who explains what actually happens after an advertisement generates a lead, and how marketing, qualification, and CS follow-up need to operate as one system.
